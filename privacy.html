Overview

Stay Pick is a lifestyle application designed to assist users in finding hotels, comparing rates, and creating shortlists of their preferred options. The privacy of our users is of utmost importance to us. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding the collection, use, and protection of your information when you utilize our app.

  • Stay Pick does not collect personal data from users.
  • The app operates offline, limiting data use.
  • User-generated data is stored exclusively on the device.

Information We Collect

Stay Pick does not collect any personal information from users. We do not require accounts, nor do we store any data that identifies you personally.

How We Use Data

Since we do not collect personal information, the app does not use user data for marketing or any other purposes. The only data processed is stored temporarily on the device to facilitate the app's functionalities.

Sharing

We do not share any personal information with third parties since we do not collect any personal data.

Analytics

Stay Pick does not use analytics to track user behavior within the app. Therefore, no analytics data is collected or shared.

Cookies/Tracking

Stay Pick does not implement cookies or tracking technologies as it does not require user accounts or collect personal information.

Data Retention

Since we do not collect or store any personal information, there is no data retention policy applicable.

Security

We employ standard industry measures to ensure the security of user data on the device. However, since no personal data is stored or transmitted, the risk of data breaches is minimal.

Children's Privacy

Stay Pick is not directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under the age of 18. If we become aware that we have collected information from a child, we will delete that information promptly.

International Users

Stay Pick is intended for users worldwide. By using our app, you acknowledge that your data may be processed in accordance with local laws, and we are committed to complying with applicable data protection regulations.

Your Rights

Users have the right to inquire about the features of the app and to request information regarding how their data is handled. Since no personal data is collected, users retain control over all data that they input into the app.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices. We encourage users to review this policy regularly for any updates.
